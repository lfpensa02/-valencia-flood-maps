⚠️
Important Notice on Satellite Limitations: The municipality of Paiporta — considered ground zero of this flood — shows relatively low detected flooding in satellite maps. This does NOT reflect the true scale of the disaster. Paiporta suffered 56 confirmed deaths, the highest of any municipality. Dense buildings and paved surfaces interfere with radar signals. These maps are a scientific tool — always interpret them alongside official emergency records and ground-level reports.

Satellites Used
Sentinel-1
SAR radar satellite. Sees through clouds day or night. Used for flood change detection between Oct 26 and Oct 31.
Landsat 8
Optical satellite by NASA/USGS. Used for NDWI water index and true color maps. Oct 30 image captured peak flood with 1.7% cloud cover.
Sentinel-2
High-resolution 10m optical satellite. Used as pre-flood visual reference for neighborhood comparisons. Sept 26 image at 0.2% cloud cover.
WorldCover
ESA land cover map at 10m resolution. Classifies land into 7 types: urban, agriculture, forest, wetland, and more.
